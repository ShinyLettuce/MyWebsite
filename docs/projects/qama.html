    Qama

    Play as Qama, the avatar of the moon god, on a quest to bring back water and life to the desert. Fight worshippers of the sun and explore to find the last remaining temple of the moon.

    Qama is a top down action exploration game where you fight your way through levels and explore to unlock the road forward.

    • Tilemap

    Level terrain was built in the game editor by filling in a three dimensional grid of blocks.

    To avoid physics based character controllers, characters were made to walk on a grid representation of the world. To create the walkable grid, the blocks placed in the editor were converted into a two dimensional tilemap where cells stored their height.

    Inspired by Oskar Stålberg on how to make visually appealing grids, the three dimensional grid was also used to generate a visual terrain that would feel less blocky.
