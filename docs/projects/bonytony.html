Bony Tony: The Revenge

Play as concrete-shoed Bony Tony and take revenge on the mafia who sunk you in the sea. Use a shotgun to blast yourself to the top of their headquarters and take out the boss.

Bony Tony is a platformer where your main mode of movement is by shooting, which launches your character in the opposite direction.

There are two different weapons that offer two unique ways of movement, a revolver and a shotgun, as well as an extra powerful shot upgrade for the shotgun.

    • Physics

    The physics for this game had to be made from scratch since our engine had no system or library in place. This meant deciding what type of collision shapes we wanted to support and making sure we got necessary data back when objects collided.

    Designing this meant deciding on some constraints to make the implementation as simple as it needed to be. Since all gameplay would happen on a singular 2D plane, this meant all physics could be checked in 2D as well. Another early decision I made was that the game would only support collisions using axis aligned bounding boxes or rays.

    I needed information from collisions such as the normal and point of collision. As such I felt the best check to use for the player was a swept AABB collision. This was done by treating the collision as a ray vs aabb check, using the Minkowski sum of both boxes. A separate test project was made where I could test and verify the math before it was implemented in the game.
